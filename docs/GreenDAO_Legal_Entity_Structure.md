# GreenDAO Legal Entity Structure

**Version**: v1.0
**Phase**: 12 (GreenDAO Launch)

## 1. The Legal Reality of DAOs
While smart contracts can govern on-chain assets (like stablecoins in a treasury), they **cannot** legally sign physical construction contracts, purchase land, or enter into Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) with utility companies.

To bridge this gap, the GreenDAO utilizes a **Hybrid Legal Wrapper** model.

## 2. The Foundation Model (Switzerland / Cayman)
For the overarching governance of the GreenDAO treasury, an independent Foundation is established (e.g., in Switzerland or the Cayman Islands).
- **Mandate**: The Foundation's legal charter strictly binds its Directors to execute the outcomes of on-chain GreenDAO votes.
- **Function**: The Foundation holds the fiat bank accounts, handles tax compliance, and signs master agreements with project developers.

## 3. Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs)
For each approved energy project, a distinct Special Purpose Vehicle (LLC) is created in the jurisdiction of the physical asset.
- **Example**: If GreenDAO votes to fund a solar farm in Texas, the Foundation creates a Texas LLC.
- The LLC signs the EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) contracts.
- Revenue generated by the LLC flows back to the Foundation, which uses it to buy-and-burn QBC or distribute yields via the QToken Engine.

## 4. Liability Protection
By utilizing this structure, individual QBC token holders are shielded from general liability regarding the physical operation of the power plants.
